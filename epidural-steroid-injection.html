Epidural Steroid Injection

Procedure

Indication

  • Selected patients with radicular pain or spinal pain where an epidural approach is appropriate.

Preparation

  • Confirm indication, consent and relevant contraindications.
  • Review imaging and identify the target level and approach.
  • Apply standard monitoring and use aseptic technique.

Procedure

  • Position the patient according to the planned approach.
  • Identify the target using appropriate imaging guidance.
  • Infiltrate the skin with local anaesthetic.
  • Advance the needle toward the target under image guidance.
  • Confirm appropriate needle position; use contrast when indicated.
  • After satisfactory placement, inject the planned epidural medication.
  • Remove the needle and apply a sterile dressing.

After Procedure

  • Observe the patient for immediate adverse effects.
  • Document the approach, level, medications and clinical response.

Important Complications

  • Dural puncture and post-dural puncture headache
  • Bleeding or epidural haematoma
  • Infection
  • Nerve injury
  • Transient increase in pain or neurological symptoms
  • Systemic effects of injected medications
